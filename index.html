About Edonbeatz

From rhythm to records.

Music has always been in me. Now I build the sounds that make people feel.

From playing drums in school and church from a young age, to learning the keyboard and eventually moving into music production, the journey has always been driven by curiosity about music and sound.

Production began in 2018, with a professional focus taking shape from 2020. Today, Edonbeatz produces, mixes and masters music for artists and creative projects across Afro-inspired sounds, Hip-Hop, Trap, Drill, Rap, Gospel, Reggae, Dancehall, EDM and more.

Whether it is an artist record, a beat, a soundtrack for a creative project or a complete production, the goal is to create something authentic, memorable and timeless.
